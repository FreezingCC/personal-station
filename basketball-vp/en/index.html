Hoop Practice — Support

Last updated: April 22, 2026 · Version 1.0

Hoop Practice is a basketball shooting practice app for Apple Vision Pro. This page provides support information, frequently asked questions, and how to contact us.

System Requirements

  • Device: Apple Vision Pro
  • OS: visionOS 26 or later
  • Space: A clear area of at least 3m × 3m is recommended
  • Permissions: Hand tracking and world sensing must be allowed

Getting Started

  1. Launch Hoop Practice from your visionOS Home View.
  2. When prompted, allow hand tracking and world sensing permissions.
  3. Face a clear wall so the app can detect it and place the virtual hoop.
  4. Make a shooting motion with your hand — the app detects and tracks each shot in real time.

FAQ

  • Shots not detected? Make sure your environment has adequate lighting and recalibrate hand tracking.
  • Hoop won't attach to the wall? Use the app in a reasonably open room with the wall clearly visible and unobstructed.
  • App crashes? Restart your device and ensure visionOS is up to date.
  • Is my data uploaded? No. All training data is stored only on your device and is never uploaded to the cloud.
