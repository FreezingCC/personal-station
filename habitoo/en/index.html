Habitoo — Support

Last updated: 2026-05-12 · Version 1.0

Habitoo is an AI habit coach for iPhone. You tell it what you want to do, and the AI shrinks the goal down to a version you'll actually do tonight. It anchors the habit to a moment you already pass through. On the 1st of every month, it writes you a review built from the dates and notes you yourself logged — no pep talks, just your own evidence. This page provides technical support information and contact details.

System Requirements

  • Device: iPhone
  • OS: iOS 17.4 or later
  • Network: AI features (goal shrinking, anchor suggestions, monthly review) require an internet connection. Other features work offline.

Getting Started

  1. Open the app and type the habit you want to build ("read more," "stretch," "meditate").
  2. The AI gives you four micro-starts — from a 30-second tiny action to a 5-minute full micro-habit. Pick one you could do tonight without hesitating.
  3. Choose an anchor (a moment to bind the habit to): either a fixed time (e.g. 07:30) or an existing action (e.g. "after your morning coffee").
  4. When the anchor moment arrives you'll get a local notification. Mark it done in Today view, optionally write a note about how it felt.
  5. On the 1st of every month the app automatically generates a review of the previous month — citing the dates and notes you wrote, calling out the best week and the failure patterns.

Known Limitations and Roadmap

  • v1.0 supports iPhone only — no iPad, Mac, or Vision Pro yet. Multi-platform is on the roadmap, timing TBD.
  • No account system, no cross-device sync, no data export in this version. These will roll out with Pro.
  • Monthly review is text-only in v1.0; a shareable visual card is a planned Pro feature.
